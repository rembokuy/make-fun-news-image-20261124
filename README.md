# The World Today — Tuesday 24 November 2026

Tuesday 24 November 2026 was a day when one boardroom decision moved the whole stock market, while wars, elections and disasters filled the rest of the front page. Nvidia's board brought Jensen Huang back as chief executive less than two weeks after he stepped down, and the shares surged about a third as the Nasdaq jumped roughly six percent. The same day brought a grim leaked-records estimate of more than 700,000 Russian soldiers killed in Ukraine, a historic Democratic majority of 258 seats in the US House after a North Carolina race decided by about 330 votes, rescuers digging through rubble in Kyiv after drone strikes killed four people, Iran defying a widening American oil blockade as tankers gathered to test it, a pause on blowing up toxic explosives stored in Iowa, and a factory fire in Banten, Indonesia, that killed 24 people and left about 30 missing. This image gathers all seven stories onto one newspaper front page, each with its own hand-drawn flat-vector illustration.

## Run it

The program only copies `entry.png` (sitting next to `main.ts`) into the output directory. Node 22, standard library only, no dependencies and no network:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

It creates the output directory if needed and prints where it copied the file.
